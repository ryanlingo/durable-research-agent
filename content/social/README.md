## How to ship a cut

1. Open the source draft; confirm `status: ready`.  
2. Paste X thread as a thread; LinkedIn as one post.  
3. Attach listed media when the platform allows.  
4. Link the repo once at the end.  
5. Do not wait on Loom; video is optional polish.

## Voice

Match `context/STYLE.md` (local): lead with the claim, no marketer filler, no em dashes. Temporal terms: [`../concepts/temporal-concepts.md`](../concepts/temporal-concepts.md).
